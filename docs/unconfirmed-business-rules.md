# Unconfirmed Business Rules (Provisional Defaults)

The following are intentionally configurable and treated as provisional:

- Report type list beyond initial defaults.
- Exact recommendation statuses and transition graph.
- Recommendation number uniqueness scope (currently per report).
- Full closure conditions beyond mandatory justification presence.
- Justification review workflow strictness and reviewers.
- Notification channels and delivery timing specifics.
- Archive policy (manual vs automatic triggers).
- Attachment handling and storage policy.
- Date display format and timezone per user/profile.
- Data retention and deletion policy for compliance records.
- Final export format requirements and columns.
- Site/legal-entity dependency enforcement details per policy.
